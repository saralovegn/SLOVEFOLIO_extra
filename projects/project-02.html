Project 02

TORRES

TORRES is a photographic project inspired by the enigmatic and powerful presence of the famous Torres Blancas in Madrid.

The project shows the brutalist architecture of the building through a series of images that play with light, shadow and reflections to create a mysterious and captivating atmosphere. The first image is a superposition of 2 different shots and angle of the building, thus creating an abstract and artistic composition.
