About me

My design philosophy

I am Sara Love, a graphic designer and illustrator that loves both sides in the art of design. I am both fascinated by minimal and sober design and by bold and more chaotic and experimental aesthetics.

My only requirement for any project is that it has a strong concept behind it, something interesting to say or communicate. Design is a powerful and social tool.
